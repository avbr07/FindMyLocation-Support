## Common questions

### The app is not showing my location

The app needs location permission to do anything at all. Open the iPhone **Settings** app →
**Privacy & Security** → **Location Services**, make sure Location Services is on, then find
**Find My Location** in the list and allow it.

Indoors, the first fix can take a minute and can be off by tens of metres. Standing near a window
or stepping outside usually resolves it.

### My address looks wrong

Addresses come from Apple's mapping data, not from us. Newly built streets and recently renumbered
properties can lag by months, and we cannot correct them from inside the app. The latitude and
longitude shown are the accurate values — if those look right and the street name does not, the map
data is the thing that is behind.

### The app stopped recording when my screen went off

Trip recording continues with the screen off only if you have turned on the background option on the
Location tab. iOS will also stop a background app if the device is very low on battery or if Low
Power Mode is on.

### Where are my trips stored?

On your phone, and only on your phone. The app keeps roughly the last week of travel so the Trips
tab has something to show, and older entries fall off automatically. Nothing is uploaded to us,
there is no account, and there is no sign-in.

### How do I move my data to a new phone?

The Settings tab can write a backup file that you keep, and read one back in later. Because the file
is yours, it goes wherever you put it — iCloud Drive, Files, or a computer.

### The step count looks wrong

Steps come from the iPhone's own motion sensor, which needs the **Motion & Fitness** permission.
Counts settle a short while after you stop walking rather than updating on every step, so a number
checked mid-stride will read low.

### Does the app work without a signal?

Your position, speed, altitude and compass all work with no data connection, because they come from
the phone's own sensors. Looking up places and weather needs a connection.
